View model assumptions, dimensions and limitations

Balanced three-phase AC feeder with Pbase = 140 kW, requested AC-side PEV = 220 kW, VLL = 400 V, power factor = 0.98, Srated = 400 kVA. I = Ptr/(√3 VLL PF). Heat losses: Ploss = 800 W + 3(0.009 Ω) I². Thermal capacitance Cθ = 1.5 MJ/K; heat conductance H = 150 W/K; θ(0) = 70°C. Ambient 42°C or 28°C held constant for a six-hour illustrative period. The 90°C criterion is chosen only for this synthetic demonstration, not a transformer manufacturer rating or regulatory safety limit.

The managed scenario uses a simple predetermined reduction to 52% of charging request at t ≥ 1.5 h. It is not an optimal policy. PEV denotes AC-side charger input and does not include DC conversion efficiency or battery dynamics. The chart's admissible set is a displayed subset of algebraic instantaneous limits, not a viability kernel. Road routing uses fixed illustrative travel times, independent of transformer state. No failure probability, field validation, uncertainty distribution or actual repair duration is inferred.

*Charging energy is an AC-side energy integral of the prescribed load, not verified energy stored in EV batteries. No operational recommendations follow from these synthetic numbers.

Research design rule: What the animation depicts must not exceed what the governing physics and evidence justify.
