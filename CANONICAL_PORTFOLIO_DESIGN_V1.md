# Canonical Portfolio Design V1

Status: GOVERNING

The visual language shown in the approved portfolio screenshot is the canonical design reference for `dossiya-se.github.io`.

## Governing visual language

- white/light research canvas;
- fine mathematical background grid;
- dark navy/charcoal typography;
- green as the primary identity and interaction color;
- deterministic mathematics architecture;
- curved mathematical relationships rather than random force layouts;
- large serif mathematical/research headings;
- compact sans-serif labels and metadata;
- persistent right-hand **Selected Family** interpretation panel on desktop;
- representative formula displayed prominently for the selected family;
- active mathematical relationships highlighted in green;
- unrelated structures reduced in opacity;
- four-level mathematical organization:
  1. Foundations
  2. Model Structures
  3. Inference + Computation
  4. Decision + Frontier

## Frozen interaction semantics

The mathematics atlas must remain deterministic. Coordinates are editorial coordinates, not proficiency scores or data-derived embeddings. Selecting a family updates its formula, topics and portfolio role, highlights mathematically informative neighbours, and de-emphasizes unrelated structures.

The interpretation boundary remains explicit: links mean mathematically informative relationships, not causal evidence or theorem-level implication.

## Refinement-only rule

Future work may improve text clipping, node spacing, line routing, alignment, responsive behavior, formula rendering, typography, accessibility and performance. It must not replace the canonical visual language without an explicit new design decision.

## Scientific boundary

Visual prominence must not strengthen scientific claims. Source mathematics, model choices, computed quantities, verified properties, empirical evidence, hypotheses and targets remain distinct.

The numerical browser demonstrator remains scientifically separate from the portfolio visual system.

## Credential boundary

The public undergraduate title remains exactly:

`BSc Physical Science in Renewable Energy and Energy System`

Ongoing graduate statuses and all existing evidence/validation boundaries remain unchanged.
