Evidence and validation

Verification is necessary; empirical validation is different

Software tests, source mathematics, model outputs, empirical observations and research hypotheses remain separate evidence states throughout the portfolio.

Claim-control invariant

\[\text{claim strength}\le\text{evidence strength}\]
  • Simulation is not relabeled as observation.
  • Passing tests does not equal field validation.
  • Source theorem does not become an original contribution.
  • Mathematical analogy requires a formally defined transfer before scientific interpretation.
