Research build

Research doctrine

Mechanisms before decoration

01

Physics before prediction

Conservation, constitutive structure, dynamic interfaces and constraints define the admissible model space.

02

Inference before certainty

Unknown parameters and interfaces are estimated with identifiability, uncertainty and measurement error made explicit.

03

Service before assets

Resilience is evaluated through critical-service continuity, recovery trajectories and consequences to populations.

04

Viability before optimum

Optimization is performed only after safety, sustainability and equity constraints define a feasible operating region.

2026 mathematical-computational frontier

Classical guarantees first; learned operators where they add measurable value

Current scientific-computing research increasingly uses operator learning and physics-informed machine learning for PDE surrogates, inverse problems and digital-twin workflows. The portfolio treats these as complementary methods, not replacements for conservation, numerical verification or uncertainty analysis.
